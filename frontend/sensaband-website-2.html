ABOUT THE PROJECT

SensaBand H₂S

Wearable H₂S and vitals monitoring with AI-based risk estimation

What is SensaBand H₂S?

SensaBand is a wearable industrial safety device built on an ESP32-C3, combining an MQ-136 hydrogen sulfide sensor with vitals sensing (heart rate, SpO2, temperature).

Readings stream in real time to a safety dashboard, where thresholds trigger alerts for supervisors to acknowledge and resolve.
